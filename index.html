Inkshelf support

Inkshelf is an offline reader for the comic and manga files (CBZ) you already own. There is no account, and the app makes no network connections. It ships with no comics, so the library starts empty until you bring your own files in.

Bring your comics in

Inkshelf opens .cbz files, and .zip files that hold page images. Any of these four ways works:

  1. The + button. In the library tap +, choose Import files… (or Import folder…) and pick the files in the Files picker.
  2. Files and the share sheet. Open a CBZ in Files, Safari downloads, Mail or any other app, tap Share and choose Inkshelf.
  3. AirDrop. Send a CBZ from a Mac or another iPhone and choose Inkshelf when asked what should open it.
  4. Finder on a Mac. Connect the iPhone, select it in Finder, open the Files tab and drag the CBZ onto Inkshelf.

Inkshelf copies each file into its own storage and groups it into a series by the series name inside the file (its ComicInfo.xml) or, when there is none, by the file name. Your original file is never changed. Pull down on the library to rescan.

Reading

Tap a cover, then a chapter. Tap the small squares at the left and right edges of the page, or swipe, to turn pages. Tap the middle of the page to show the controls. The Reader options menu at the top right changes the orientation (horizontal pages or a vertical scroll for webtoons) and, for horizontal pages, the direction (right to left for manga, left to right for comics). Each series remembers your page.

Try it with a free sample

Not sure what to open first? The samples page has free comics you can download and open in Inkshelf, with their licences and credits.

Backups

Comics you import are copied into Inkshelf's own storage and are backed up with your iPhone (iCloud or a computer backup). Libraries that arrive with a series.json index file, the layout some conversion tools write, are treated as re-creatable and are not backed up, so keep your own copy of those files.

Questions or problems?

Open an issue on GitHub Issues (github.com/Jonathan-Nuno/inkshelf-site/issues). Please tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing. Issues on GitHub are public, so do not post personal information or files you do not want to share.
