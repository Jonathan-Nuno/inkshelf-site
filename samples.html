Sample comics

Four free CBZ files for trying Inkshelf. Each one is used under an open licence, listed with its credit line and source below.

On an iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap a download link and choose Download, open the file from Safari's downloads list, then tap Share and choose Inkshelf. You can also save the file to Files and, in Inkshelf, tap + and choose Import files….

Hokusai Manga, volume 5

Sketches by Katsushika Hokusai (葛飾北斎), a printed sketchbook from the Edo period: shrines, temples, bells, figures from history and legend. It reads right to left, so it opens that way, and the scan's pages are double-page spreads. Licence: CC0 1.0 / No Copyright – United States, as stated by the Smithsonian Libraries.
